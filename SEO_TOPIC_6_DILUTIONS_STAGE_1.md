# SEO Topic 6: Dilutions — Stage 1

## Page updated

`/calculator/dilutions`

## Purpose

Turn the Dilutions category page into a stronger topic landing page for dilution and reconstitution searches before individual dilution calculators are reviewed.

## SEO targets

- dilution calculator
- C1V1 C2V2 calculator
- C1V1 formula
- reconstitution calculator
- final concentration calculator
- medication dilution calculator
- vial dose to mL calculator
- drug reconstitution calculator
- nursing dilution calculator

## Main changes

- Strengthened metadata and Open Graph/Twitter descriptions.
- Expanded calculator selection cards with best-use cases and formula snippets.
- Added formula cards for:
  - `C1 × V1 = C2 × V2`
  - `V1 = (C2 × V2) ÷ C1`
  - `Concentration = total drug ÷ final volume`
  - `mL = ordered dose ÷ concentration`
- Added step-by-step dilution calculation guidance.
- Added common dilution examples.
- Added a common mistakes section focused on final volume, diluent volume, unit consistency, and medicine preparation safety.
- Expanded the FAQ list and added FAQ schema.
- Added breadcrumb schema and strengthened CollectionPage schema.
- Added visible references section.

## Safety positioning

The page now states that dilution calculators check arithmetic only and do not confirm compatibility, stability, route, infusion rate, storage, aseptic technique, or clinical appropriateness.
